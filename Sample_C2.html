Topic T1 · First Impression

What You'd Notice Picking This Up Cold

Item C2 is a small black plastic plug with a hexagonal socket in its face, and a white band of sealant along its threads. Four things you can spot before you ever read the part number:

Left: the plug as shown in the catalog. Right: the same style of plug with the white sealant on its threads (click to enlarge).
  1. Hex socket — the hexagonal recess in the face of the plug. A hex key drives it in.
  2. Pipe thread — 1/2"-14 threads, 14 per inch, that screw into a matching port.
  3. Black HDPE — the body is molded from black high density polyethylene plastic.
  4. White sealant — a band of 3M Thread Sealant 4291 on the threads.

Topic T4 · Material & Grade

Black High Density Polyethylene (HDPE)

The “HDPE” in the item description calls out the material. Here’s what it means.

Requirement

Material: Plastic — High Density Polyethylene (HDPE), Black

Where It Comes From

This type of plastic is obtained from petroleum or natural gas sources.

What the Grade Means

HDPE has a density greater than or equal to 0.941 g/cm³, which gives it greater rigidity and resistance.

Reference

Glossary

Every code, acronym, and standard used in this module, defined in plain language. Tap a term's "jump" button to see it in context.

3M Thread Sealant 4291

A white, flowable liquid sealant (59% solid, viscosity 400–1200 cps) applied in the threads of this plug. The “4291” in the part number refers to it.

Catalog Part Number

A part number taken from a supplier’s catalog. The leading diagonal in /NIAGRTH7/4291 marks this part as catalog based.

Dim B / Dim C

Dimension columns in the Niagara TH Series table. For TH-7, Dim B and Dim C are both 0.38 in. Dim B is also the dimension marked across the hex socket drawing.

HDPE

High Density Polyethylene — a plastic obtained from petroleum or natural gas, with a density of 0.941 g/cm³ or more, which gives it greater rigidity and resistance.

Hex Socket

A drive with a hexagonal recess in the face of the part, turned with a hex key.

Niagara (NIAGR)

The catalog supplier behind this item; the TH Series threaded plugs come from the Niagara catalog.

NPT

National Pipe Taper — the standard tapered pipe thread. This plug protects NPT threads.

Pipe Thread

A thread type that lets pipes and fittings screw together into a secure, robust connection.

TH Series

The Niagara catalog line of threaded plugs. TH-7 is the 1/2"-14 size.

TPI

Threads per inch — how many thread crests fit in one inch. This part is 14 TPI.
