Inspection Report

When a lot is inspected, the dimensions and the evaluation of the part are recorded on a copy of the template. That copy is the Inspection Report, found in SBS Connect for review — for example after a customer complaint.

The complete inspection report for /ROTORHC18STCS2 — header, print drawing and feature table in one view.

Hover over a number on the report to see what is checked and where that dimension comes from on the Rotor Clip print.

1 · Burrs / Thread Aesthetic

Visual check. No caliper reading and no numeric tolerance: the inspector looks at each sample for burrs and surface defects.

2 · Material

SAE 1060-1075 steel (Ref = Steel), checked visually and against documents, not measured. It matches Material = Steel in the item specification (T3).

3 · Surface Finish / Color

SILVER DORRLTECH, checked visually and against documents. The supplier finish code CS2 is Magni 565 (Silver), see T5.

4 · Width over Tangs – W

Max 1.620 in, measured with calipers. W is the width dimension on the Rotor Clip print drawing, and 1.620 is the same figure as Band Width in the item specification (T3).

5 · Length of Tangs – F

Min 0.344 / Max 0.406 in, measured with calipers. F is the length of the bent tabs on the print drawing.

6 · Wire Diameter – E

Ref 0.152 in, measured with calipers and recorded pass/fail. E is the wire diameter on the print drawing.

7 · Overall Height – Y

Ref 2.00 in, measured with calipers and recorded pass/fail. Y is the overall height of the clamp on the print drawing.

Reference

Glossary

Every code and term used in this module, defined in plain language. Tap a term's "jump" button to see it in context.

Document Links

The Item Maintenance tab that lists an item's controlled documents, such as the inspection report template.

FMEA

Failure Mode and Effects Analysis — a structured review of how a process could fail, included in a Level 4 PPAP.

HC-18

Rotor Clip's own part number for this hose clamp — the “HC18” in the part number.

Hose Clamp

A clamp that holds a hose onto a fitting. This one is a single-wire clamp made from one piece of bent wire.

Magni 565 (Silver)

The silver finish called out by “CS2”. The SBS website lists it as Silver Dorrltech.

Nominal Diameter

The named size of the clamp. In 1.125 SINGLE WIRE HOSE CLAMP, 1.125 is the nominal diameter in inches.

Non-standard Part

A part whose item number starts with a slash, marking it as a non-standard item.

P21

SBS's ERP system, where item records, document links, classes and locations are kept.

PPAP

Production Part Approval Process — the documentation package that shows a supplier's process consistently meets customer requirements. This item is Class 5: PPAP Level 4.

Rotor Clip

The supplier named in the part number (“ROTOR”), known for retaining rings and hose clamps.

VMI (Vendor-Managed Inventory)

A bin system where SBS manages and restocks parts at the customer's location. This module is named after Item A2's cell on the VMI bin card.
