Get to know your Sample Box.

Handle some of the parts SBS carries, with the structures they actually have. Inside are 32 different part numbers, two pieces of each, in their own labeled compartments.

How it works

Every card matches a compartment in the real box. Use it to double-check a part before you grab it, or to identify an unlabeled one.

  1. 1

    Find the spot

    Match the letter (column) and number (row) on the box sides, or the lid label, to the same spot here.

  2. 2

    Hover to flip

    Each card shows a part number and description. Hover, or tap on a phone, to flip it for a real photo.

  3. 3

    Read the card

    The part number and description tell you what you need to know. Lid labels carry part numbers too, some with a short description.

Why we made this box

No engineering background needed, just a friendly way to get familiar with the parts we sell.

  • 32 real parts

    Handle the materials, finishes, and dimensions in person, not just pictures on a screen.

  • Learn at your pace

    A hands-on way for anyone at SBS, new or experienced, to learn each part's category and how to identify it.

  • Use it anytime

    Come back whenever you need a quick refresher. No need to memorize everything up front.

  • Helps us grow

    Shows how the team's part knowledge is growing, so we know where more training can help.
